Su-27 Radio Pilot Manual

How to fly with the Su-27 Radio (WW) add-on v1.1 in the Flaming Cliffs Su-27 and J-11A.

Before you fly

You need DCS World with the Su-27 or J-11A from Flaming Cliffs, and SRS for the radios. Without SRS everything works in the cockpit, but nothing gets tuned.

Install

  1. Unzip the download so the folder sits at Saved Games\DCS\Mods\tech\Su-27 Radio (WW).
  2. Double-click INSTALL.bat in that folder. It copies su27radio_hook.lua into Saved Games\DCS\Scripts\Hooks and the example cartridge into Saved Games\DCS\DTC\Su-27, and adds the radio controls to the Su-27's controls files in the DCS install folder (the J-11A uses the same files).
  3. Start DCS. Options > Special > Su-27 Radio has the on / off switch and a debug log.

A DCS update or repair puts ED's controls files back, and the radio controls disappear from Options > Controls: run INSTALL.bat again. Your key assignments are kept by DCS and come back with them. If the installer can't find your DCS folder, run INSTALL.bat -DcsPath "D:\DCS World" from a command prompt; if Windows refuses the change (DCS in Program Files), right-click INSTALL.bat > Run as administrator.

Remove: UNINSTALL.bat takes the hook and the controls out. Then delete the folder. Your cartridges stay.

Power

The plate and the radio windows are dark until the jet has electric power: the electric power switch, or an engine running. When power comes on they run a short lamp test (88 in every window, both lamps lit), then both radios are tuned to the channels on their КАНАЛ knobs and the volumes are set.

Hot and air starts begin with power on.

The ВВОД ДАННЫХ plate

On the right console wall, beside your right hip, on what used to be a blank cover.

  • КАССЕТА knob: МИС = the mission's radio presets, 1 to 6 = cartridges. Left click turns it anticlockwise (toward МИС), right click clockwise.
  • ЗАГР button: loads what the КАССЕТА knob is on. The В РАБОТЕ window flashes "--" while the cartridge is read.
  • ГОТОВ (green): loaded. The window shows П0 for the mission's presets or П1 to П6 for a cartridge.
  • ОТКАЗ (red): there is no cartridge file for that number, or it can't be read. The cartridge loaded before stays in.

At the start of a mission the mission's presets are in (П0). After every load, both radios go to the channels on their КАНАЛ knobs, and the channel card is written out again.

The УКВ / КВ radio panel

On the left console. Two radios, each with its channel knob, channel window and volume knob:

  • УКВ: the R-800, SRS radio 1, 100–399.975 MHz.
  • КВ: the R-864, SRS radio 2, 3–10 MHz.

КАНАЛ: the big knob. Left click = channel down, right click = channel up, 1 to 20 and round again. The window above it shows the channel. Every step tunes SRS to that channel of what is loaded. A channel with no frequency in it: the window still shows the number, and SRS stays where it was.

ГРОМК: the small knob beside it. The radio's volume in SRS, in 20 steps: left click quieter, right click louder. The pointer goes from 7:30 (silent) clockwise to 4:30 (full). Both start at 80 %. The knob wins over SRS's own volume slider for these two radios.

Controls (new in 1.1): Options > Controls > Su-27 (or J-11A), category Su-27 Radio (WW), has channel up / down and volume up / down for both radios (UKV = R-800, KV = R-864). Nothing is bound to start with. One press is one step, the knob turns with it and SRS follows, as with a click. The plate has no controls: it is used on the ground.

SRS takes the commands on its UDP command port, 9040 by default (SRS client settings).

The channel card

ED's КАНАЛЫ РАДИОСВЯЗИ card on the right canopy rail is filled in by hand, in blue ink, from what is loaded:

  • The channels that have a frequency, with their numbers: УКВ first (01, 02 …), then КВ (К 1, К 2 …). 20 rows in two halves.
  • Frequency in MHz, the station and the callsign. A cartridge has them written in; with МИС they come from the mission, and your group's own frequency is "своя группа".
  • Top right: "кассета 2 - <name>" for a cartridge, "МИС - задание" for the mission's presets.

More than 20 channels in one cartridge: the card shows the first 20, the radios still tune all of them.

Cartridges

A cartridge is a small text file in Saved Games\DCS\DTC\Su-27, named <number> <name>.dtc, for example 1 Example comm plan.dtc. The first digit is the КАССЕТА position that loads it, so keep one file per number.

Make them with the DTC Planner, or write one by hand:

{
  "type": "Su-27 DTC", "version": 1, "name": "Example comm plan", "terrain": "Caucasus",
  "ukv": [ {"ch": 1, "f": 251.500, "station": "Полёт", "call": "Витязь"} ],
  "kv":  [ {"ch": 1, "f": 3.500, "station": "Дальняя связь", "call": "Утёс"} ]
}

ukv channels 1–20 at 100–399.975 MHz, kv channels 1–20 at 3–10 MHz. Station and callsign are only for the card; Cyrillic works.

МИС, the mission's presets: the radio presets set for your aircraft in the mission editor (radio 1 = УКВ, radio 2 = КВ). A slot without presets gets its group's frequency on УКВ channel 1. This needs the hook that INSTALL.bat copies.

Using the DTC Planner

Open it online, or open DTC Planner\WW DTC Planner.html from the download in any browser (it works offline). Pick Su-27 / J-11A at the top; the same planner makes MiG-29A(WW) and F-15C SIT (WW) cartridges.

  • КАССЕТА tab: the cartridge's name and number; the file name it will be saved as.
  • УКВ R-800 and КВ R-864: 20 channels each, with frequency, station (up to 15 letters) and callsign (up to 13). With "Russian names" ticked, Latin typed into a station or callsign turns into Cyrillic (Lastochka → Ласточка); words in capitals stay as typed.
  • Airfields: click one on the map, or pick it from the list, for its DCS tower frequencies. One click puts VHF or UHF into the first free УКВ channel and HF into КВ, with the airfield's name and callsign. Airfields on the card get their channel numbers on the map.
  • Import .miz: pick a Su-27 / J-11A flight from a mission file; the lead's presets become the channels, labelled where they match an airfield.
  • КАРТОЧКА: the card as the cockpit will write it; Enlarge shows it full size.
  • Save cartridge downloads <number> <name>.dtc: move it into Saved Games\DCS\DTC\Su-27.

Options and troubleshooting

  • Nothing new in the cockpit: check Options > Special > Su-27 Radio is on, and that the folder is exactly Saved Games\DCS\Mods\tech\Su-27 Radio (WW).
  • SRS doesn't follow: SRS must be running and connected, and its UDP command port must be 9040.
  • МИС has no channels: run INSTALL.bat (the hook), and check the mission has radio presets for your aircraft. Logs\su27radio_hook.log says what it found.
  • ОТКАЗ: no file starting with that number in DTC\Su-27, or the file is broken. Open it in the planner to check.
  • No "Su-27 Radio (WW)" category in Controls: run INSTALL.bat again (after every DCS update or repair) and read what it says about the controls.
  • Logs: Saved Games\DCS\Logs\su27radio.log; turn on the debug log in Options for more detail.
